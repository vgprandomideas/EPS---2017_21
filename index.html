Manufacturing and investments

Tata Electronics

Precision mobile-phone enclosures in Krishnagiri; 18,250 prospective jobs. The January cabinet approval cited ₹5,763 crore, whereas the February assistance agreement cited ₹4,684 crore. These are different reported stages, not additive investments. Production and actual employment require separate records.

Connectivity and transport

Initiated · 2020
118.9 km

Chennai Metro Phase II

Three proposed corridors extending rail access across metropolitan Chennai. Foundation ceremony occurred in November 2020; construction and opening span later administrations. Distinct from the operational Phase I network.

Supporting record
Proposed / litigation
—

Chennai–Salem expressway

National highway corridor intended to shorten connections to western Tamil Nadu. Supreme Court judgment of December 2020 dealt with acquisition notifications; a July 2024 parliamentary answer confirms litigation and clearances prevented further processing. Not a completed road.

Supporting record
Initiated; later opened
—

Kilambakkam bus terminal

Southern intercity bus terminal intended to reduce congestion at Koyambedu. Construction began during the period; opening occurred in December 2023. Exact original sanction requires CMDA records.

Planning / site selection
—

Chennai second airport

A second airport was discussed during the tenure. Parandur was selected after the tenure; land acquisition, approvals and construction are separate milestones. No operational second airport attributable to 2017–21.

Programme
—

Bus fleet modernisation

Replacement of ageing public buses and proposed cleaner fleets were part of transport investment. Distinguish procurement orders, deliveries and fleet additions; exact year-wise verified fleet count pending.

Eleven government medical colleges

Joint scheme covering Virudhunagar, Namakkal, Nilgiris, Tiruppur, Tiruvallur, Nagapattinam, Dindigul, Kallakurichi, Ariyalur, Ramanathapuram and Krishnagiri. Estimated ₹4,000 crore; Union contribution ₹2,145 crore. Later inauguration does not negate earlier approvals.
